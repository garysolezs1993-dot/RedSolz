# Sonilo prompts for the Common Wolf

Use **Sound Effect → Quick SFX**, generate one clip at a time, preview it, then click **Save Audio File**. Keep the current procedural files until all three replacements sound right. The generator bills by generated audio duration.

## Howl

Category: Creature Sound

Prompt: `One isolated adult wolf howl for a dark fantasy 2D game. A single rough, resonant, mournful howl that rises slightly then falls and wavers, with a breathy start and short clean release. About 1.1 seconds total, one voice only. Dry close-miked game sound, clear mid-low wolf tone. No music, wind, forest ambience, footsteps, extra growls, echo, or long reverb tail. One-shot, not a loop.`

## Chase huff

Category: Creature Sound

Prompt: `One short close predator huff for a wolf running in a dark fantasy 2D game. A breathy low guttural exhale with a little throat rasp, about 0.25 seconds, quick onset and soft short tail. Quiet and restrained so it can repeat during a chase. Dry one-shot, no howl, no bark, no music, no footsteps, no background ambience, no long reverb.`

## Attack

Category: Monster Growl

Prompt: `One short aggressive wolf bite-attack vocal for a dark fantasy 2D action game. Immediate low snarling growl that snaps into a sharp raspy bark, with a forceful airy burst, about 0.3 seconds total and a very short tail. Distinct from a long howl. Dry close-miked one-shot; no impact sound, no music, no ambience, no echo, no long reverb.`
