# Cursed Wolf replacement animation sheets

Source: `cursed_wolf_boss_animation_sheets_for_codex.zip` supplied by the user.

The original sheets are opaque RGB art with black/checkerboard backgrounds and panel borders. `*_original.png` preserves those originals. `*_clean.png` is a transparent cutout derivative prepared with the built-in imagegen tool (two passes: background extraction, then padding). Small edge details may differ from the original; these are not pixel-identical extractions.

Runtime loads 8 frames each from `../walk_v3`, `../jump_v3`, `../pounce_v3`, and `../attack_v3`. Howl, howl AOE, death, collision, combat damage and timings are retained. Frames use 640x512 canvases, nearest-neighbor sampling, one constant scale per track, and paw anchor row 439. Attack energy can extend below that anchor. `tools/prepare_cursed_wolf_v3.py` reproduces the slicing/packing; edit its PAW_ROWS for individual-frame ground corrections.

Each equal cell must contain exactly one COMPLETE wolf, with generous 24-pixel transparent margins on ALL sides, including tail, paws and effect tips. Restore only any tips visibly cut by the original panel boundaries. Keep the anatomical head/torso size consistent across eight frames; do not scale individual poses to fill cells. Preserve the gait/attack differences between each pose. No pose may touch a cell border or overlap a neighboring cell. Align grounded paws on a shared baseline near 80% cell height. Preserve fine edge pixels without gray boxes or white halos. Clean cutout editing, not a new character design. No text of any kind.

Every wolf plus ALL energy/embers MUST fit inside the central 70% width of its cell, with clearly EMPTY transparent left and right margins at least 15% of cell width.
